How to obtain each Pokemon
--------------------------
MissingNo.: Rare spawn on beaches
Ghost: Can be found in spooky biomes at night
Glitchlett: Can spawn rarely underground
Nightmare: Use a netherite scrap on a male Haunter
S♪REN: Ultra-rare spawn in flowery biomes at night
DISABLED: Level up a Jigglypuff to 22 at night
Charon: Use a reaper cloth on a male Kirlia
Pinkachu: Give a Pichu pink concrete
Ryu: Level up a male dratini to 55 in a frozen biome
EEeEE: Use a shiny stone on an Eevee
Freakachu: Use an ice stone on a Pichu
Unown King: Ultra-rare spawn in mountain biomes at any time
Eerie Ditto: Rare spawn anywhere in the Overworld (same as normal Ditto)
Eerie Umbreon: Use an X-Attack on Eevee at night
Eerie Paras: Rare spawn in forests
Amanita: Level up Eerie Paras to 24 while holding an Everstone
Bad Egg: Ultra-rare spawn on beaches
Manafetus: Level up the Bad Egg to 50
Turmur: Ultra-rare spawn in swamps
Galactimon: Use a Moon Stone on Lunatone or a Sun Stone on Solrock
Faceleech: Rare spawn in plains biomes
Eerie Golduck: Defeat a Persian with a male Psyduck holding a Quick Claw
731: Rare spawn in villages
Mishanter: Ultra-rare spawn in jungle biomes
Cofawer: Ultra-rare spawn in forest biomes at night
Learow: Rare spawn in spooky biomes at night
Poli: Use an iron sword on Poliwhirl
FUtiLE: Ultra-rare spawn in hills and temperate biomes
Eerie Scyther: Rare spawn in forest and hills biomes, uncommon in jungle biomes
Eerie Unown: Rare spawn in the End
X32763: Ultra-rare spawn in Stony Peaks
